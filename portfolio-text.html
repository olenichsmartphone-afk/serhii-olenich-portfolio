Launching an AI microdrama slate

Audience growth · 2026 · Microdrama · Organic growth · Episodic IP

32.68M views · ~99K followers

Built original AI series channels from zero and found an audience across Instagram and TikTok in about one month.

Contribution

Researched formats, tested hooks, built recurring characters, wrote and produced episodes, and used performance data to decide which stories to continue.

Results

Six episodes across four shows in the first three weeks. By 25 November 2026, the accounts had ~99,000 combined followers. Dashboard readouts totalled 32.681M views: 13.981M Instagram and 18.7M TikTok. The two platforms also recorded 1.288M combined likes, 48.2K shares and 11.7K comments. Instagram separately recorded 69.7K saves, 10.9K reposts, 1.576M profile visits and 1,350 link taps. TikTok recorded 1.4M profile views.

Source: public profile counts and creator-reported Instagram and TikTok Insights, 25 November 2026. TikTok activity was reported for 21 October–24 November. The Instagram reporting window was not captured. Counts aggregate platform activity.

Roomora in an original microdrama

Branded entertainment · 2026 · Microdrama · Brand integration · Retention

~2.2M views on the earlier campaign snapshot

Produced a story-led Roomora integration that reached more than two million views across four placements in an earlier campaign snapshot.

Contribution

Wrote Roomora into the plot of an original episode, published the integration across Instagram and TikTok, and tracked retention and audience responses.

Results

An earlier snapshot showed about 2.2M views across four placements. A later public snapshot on 25 November showed about 3.3M combined views across two visible uploads. In one measured upload, 61% of viewers reached the end of the integration at about 00:48. When asked in a follow-up under the next episode, one viewer recalled Roomora after about four days.

The two view counts cover different sets of posts and dates. The creator separately reports about 50% completion and 10–20% skip rates on roughly 90-second series Reels. Those figures describe the series, while the 61% applies to one upload. The recall example came from a prompted comment. Conversion data was unavailable.
